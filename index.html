      ✦A safe place to keep your dreams

      Every member's passion is safe, respected and celebrated here.

      Yume Sanctuary is a non-profit that creates safe, fun and interactive events for the youth of Digos. The Dreamers are our cosplay community: Digos cosplayers of every level.

      Why we exist

      ☾

      Hobbies still face judgment

      Cosplay and other interests are not yet widely accepted, so many young people hide what they love.

      ➤

      Events are far from home

      Davao and GenSan are the usual destinations, costing Digos hobbyists hours of travel and a lot of money.

      ⌂

      Digos needs its own place

      Digos has the fans but no year-round, community-run place built for them to meet, play and grow. They deserve a sanctuary close to home.

      Our vision

      A Digos where every young person can dream freely and pursue what they love, with friends and without judgment, in a community strong enough to sustain itself.
      SafeLocalSelf-sustaining

      Digos as the regional hub

      Today

      Digos hobbyists travel to Davao or GenSan, spending time and money to attend events.

      Our goal

      Digos hosts the events, so the community and its visitors come to us.

      From one week to year-round

      1

      Start small

      • Small, regular events with local business partners
      • Code of conduct and safety team in place
      • A core team of volunteers
      2

      Make Digos a hub

      • Recurring TCG, Pokémon, cosplay, Beyblade and Tekken events
      • Draw hobbyists from nearby cities
      • Partner with schools and local government
      3

      Sustain ourselves

      • Community-funded events with no sponsor needed
      • Local youth trained to lead and organize
      • Digos known as the region's hobby-event hub

      A sanctuary you can trust

      ✦

      Clear code of conduct

      No harassment, bullying or hate. Posted and enforced at every event.

      ☾

      Safe reporting

      A simple, private way to raise concerns, and we act on them.

      ♡

      Open to every newcomer

      The path to self-sustaining

      ①

      Partner-supported

      Local businesses such as Minna and Maivan help us host the first events.

      ②

      Community-funded

      Ticket fees, vendor booths, merch and memberships begin to cover costs.

      ③

      Self-sustaining

      The community funds its own events, with no sponsor required.

      Our long-term test: can the community run an event without outside sponsors?
